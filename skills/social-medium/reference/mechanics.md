## Overview

Use this reference for publication economics, evidence interpretation, and formats requiring an interface check. Keep platform observations separate from editorial choices. An aggregate association doesn't promise an individual story's outcome.

## Publication and earnings decisions

| Decision | Verified mechanism and action |
|---|---|
| Enroll in the Partner Program | Enrollment reopened with immediate access for members aged 18 or older who have at least one published story, a photo and bio, and a bank in an eligible payout country. Check the live eligible-country list before promising access; don't add a follower minimum or application wait. (official, Enrollment, 2026-08) |
| Explain editorial incentives | The Editor Partner Program opened to all qualifying publications. Assigned editors receive 25% of the paywalled story's earnings from a separate budget; this isn't a deduction from the writer. (official, Editor program, 2026-07) |
| Evaluate publication quality | Qualification considers overall distribution levels over time. Don't invent a publication-wide penalty triggered by a single flagged story. (official, Editor program, 2026-07) |
| Explain payout | Earnings combine qualifying member reading/listening time with engagement, including claps, highlights and replies. Read-ratio adjustments, Boost, and acquisition/conversion bonuses also matter. Views alone cannot predict earnings. (official, Earnings, 2026-09) |
| Plan external discovery | Qualifying external member reads receive a 5% bonus; search and subscriber-email discovery have additional allocations, and a new-member conversion can earn a one-time payment. Don't extend this to every external visitor. (official, Earnings, 2026-09) |
| Choose free or paywalled | Medium's interview reports roughly two-thirds of monthly stories were free. This describes publication volume, without establishing a higher-quality paywalled audience or a recommended free/paywall quota. (official, Worth it, 2026-02) |
| Share paywalled proof | Authors and publication editors can provide Friend Links granting free access. Paying-member reads still earn; nonmember reads through those links don't. Use this access path where the buyer needs to inspect the whole case. (official, Friend Links, 2026-09) |
| Build a professional portfolio | Medium's interview describes expertise writing as a route to professional opportunities and recommends syndication and audience development beyond Partner Program income. Treat jobs or consulting as possible outcomes, with no earnings forecast. (official, Worth it, 2026-02) |

Don't change a free launch explanation into paywalled content just to satisfy an editor's preferences. Choose a different publication if its controls conflict with the audience's access needs. (directional)

## Evidence that changes publishing choices

| Observation | Bound and writing decision |
|---|---|
| Most discovery involves people | More than 75% of stories in the average feed result from actions such as publication acceptance or reader reposts, including staff Boost and followed-writer publishing. Write something a reader can recommend specifically; don't reduce all distribution to Boost. (official, Curation era, 2026-07) |
| Reposts attract clicks | Medium reported over 250,000 reposts and three-times-higher clicks than other engagement-sharing signals. This is platform aggregate evidence; request optional contextual recommendations only from readers who mean them. (measured, Repost notes, n=over 250,000 reposts, 2026-07) |
| Clicks and reading can diverge | Repost clickers read stories about 28% more often than people clicking for other feed reasons. The same newsletter says repost viewers “stay” about 36% less often than average readers, without a sufficiently defined stay metric. Don't translate that into retention or completion; align the note's promise with the opening and inspect your own reading evidence. (measured, Writer newsletter, n=Medium repost traffic with sample undisclosed, 2026-08) |
| Subscription design matters | A follow/subscribe redesign produced a reported 30-fold subscriber increase. This is a product experiment, not a writer-level growth forecast. Make the ongoing subject of the writer newsletter clear; observe subscriber change without attributing every change to the latest story. (measured, Worth it, n=Medium design experiment with sample undisclosed, 2026-02) |
| Topic placement helps discovery | Moving topic pills to the top increased topic follows and subsequent reads in early results; no sample or lift was published. Select accurate topics and don't invent topic-level analytics when they aren't exposed. (measured, Writer newsletter, n=Medium design experiment with sample undisclosed, 2026-08) |
| Sharing can grow the sharer's audience | Logged-in readers arriving through externally shared Medium links can receive a prompt to follow the sharer. Let a credible contributor explain why the story matters in their own voice; this doesn't guarantee writer follows. (official, Writer newsletter, 2026-08) |
| Publication acceptance isn't a reach guarantee | ILLUMINATION reported about 10,000 June stories, a million views, and 380,000 reads, with uneven story reach. Don't turn a publication aggregate into a target for an individual draft. (measured, ILLUMINATION update, n=about 10,000 stories in that publication, 2026-07) |
| A long personal story can hold interest | A publication editor reported a 76% read rate for a nine-minute confessional. It is a self-selected anecdote, not a length study. Use real experience when relevant; don't fabricate confession or infer an optimal length. (measured, Editor essay, n=one self-selected story, 2026-04) |
| Long guides need navigation | Medium reports about one in ten stories exceeds 2,000 words or ten minutes; structured stories receive an automatic web table of contents with no toggle. Neither the prevalence figure nor the feature sets a recommended word band. Use descriptive headings and check the preview. (official, Long reads, 2026-07) |
| Search remains an access route | Public free and paywalled stories remain crawlable; Medium reports declining traditional-search traffic alongside rising AI-search referrals. Keep source attribution and useful explanations; don't promise years of traffic or infer a new ranking recipe. (official, AI/search controls, 2026-03) |

The interview's feed CTR bands are commentary, not a controlled benchmark: it cites 2–3% as potentially unclear presentation and 7–10% as strong. The Reader tips page supports topic-sensitive interpretation. Never prescribe a rewrite from these bands alone. (official, Worth it, 2026-02; official, Reader tips, 2026-03)

For diagnosis, get the actual Stats display and its field definitions. Record presentations and matching feed views only if exposed; keep external views separate. A useful working record includes the story, reporting interval, title/preview change, fields supplied, and relevant reader questions. Record missing fields as unavailable. Review qualified destination actions in the destination's analytics. These are measurement practices, not promises about dashboard fields. (directional)

## Publication controls and format preparation

| Format or control | Prepare and verify |
|---|---|
| Submission topics | Editors can define up to ten publication topics. Compare the draft against the publication's About-page topics; missing or unmatched topics can receive badges without automatic rejection. (official, Off-topic badge, 2026-06) |
| Live submission gate | Check required subtitle, preview image, topic overlap, paywall status, and minimum reading time before submission. Match the publication's real requirements instead of guessing from other publications. (official, Submission controls, 2026-07) |
| Decline email | Read the optional editorial reason if supplied. Don't assume a fixed taxonomy of rejection reasons. (official, Writer newsletter, 2026-08) |
| Imported story | Import the owned-source URL; verify backdating and the automatically assigned canonical. Review any stale claims before syndicating. Don't advertise an old article as a new launch merely because it is newly imported. (official, Import, 2026-09) |
| Writer newsletter | Prepare a subject line and accurate description of the ongoing topic. Inspect the account's subscription and sending options; verify the recipient and paywall experience. Don't assume every story is emailed or promise a particular newsletter composer. (directional) |
| Publication newsletter | Supply the editor with the story link and a short reader-benefit summary. Agree on inclusion and timing; don't assume submission grants newsletter placement. Check actual sending controls with the editor. (directional) |
| Lists and series | Medium documents Lists as browsable portfolios. Group related proof, provide an entry-point story, and link the collection from About. (official, Self-promotion guide, 2025-11) Make installments independently useful and update links when a later result revises an earlier assumption. (directional) |
| Code blocks, gists, embeds | Supply copyable text for the central example. Preview code formatting; check that a gist or external embed loads without credentials and include an ordinary link fallback. State environment and prerequisites from the artifact. Avoid screenshots as the sole copy of code. Confirm current editor support before specifying insertion steps. (directional) |
| Cover and inline images | Show the actual object or evidence where available. Prepare a mobile crop, descriptive alt text, and a caption explaining what the image establishes. Cite the creator and verify rights. Identify AI-generated images in their captions as Medium requires; keep illustration separate from observed results. (official, AI policy; packaging: directional) |
| Audio or AI narration | Provide a text equivalent and document synthetic provenance. Check the current controls and applicable disclosure requirements before promising an audio feature, voice option, or monetization outcome. (directional) |
| Short updates and repost notes | Use a compact story only if it stands on its own. The verified short recommendation surface is a repost note of up to 280 characters in followers' For You feeds; don't invent another short-post product. (official, Repost notes, 2026-07) |

## Continuing proof after launch

Use a series when a real decision remains unresolved. Ajora's co-founder documented pivots and a first paying customer in a recurring Medium narrative; the signup result in that account came from an earlier LinkedIn post. Keep the source of acquisition distinct from the later story describing it. (directional) Source: Ajora case, 2026-05.

Plan a useful recap even when a launch misses its hoped-for ranking. Honoramma's founder described the later Medium article as the most valuable launch output. Treat that as personal testimony, with no general revenue claim. (directional) Source: Honoramma case, 2026-03.

For an ongoing Medium campaign, reuse the underlying product problem through materially new evidence and objections. Bundle minor updates when they don't justify a full story. This adapts Oakley's launch-campaign advice to Medium; it has no measured Medium cadence advantage. (directional) Source: Oakley, 2026-03.

Keep campaign URL fields consistent and use `utm_content` to distinguish the artifact link from the closing CTA. Google documents those attribution parameters; no parameter establishes causality. (official, Google URL builder, 2026-09) Preserve unknown sources when destination records can't establish attribution. (directional)
