## Launch operating notes

Sendible's self-reported account case associated a move away from recycled corporate posts with higher views and described a shift from weekly to daily publishing. Several practices changed together, so no isolated format or frequency effect follows (directional; Sendible). Use its practical lessons as hypotheses:

- Build participation before a launch; choose a cadence the operator can sustain.
- Prepare evergreen context ahead of time, while leaving response time unscheduled.
- Agree on themes, boundaries, and who can answer promptly.
- Test plain proof assets and link placement against the intended action. Weaker linked and polished-image posts on this account don't prove universal suppression.

These operational choices are practitioner guidance (directional; Sendible).

The DM Sales Gal episode describes a Threads discovery program and a founder becoming known for a focused topic, with conversations moving elsewhere for sales. Its promotional notes supply no outcome totals for the program and don't isolate causal revenue contribution (directional; DM Sales Gal). For the Threads portion, turn recurring buyer questions into public answers and make any consensual contact handoff explicit. Keep cross-platform sequencing outside this playbook (directional).

Tag campaign URLs consistently; Google documents source, medium, campaign, and creative distinctions (official, Google URL Builder, 2026-09 validation; undated). HockeyStack documents grouping raw self-reported source answers into a channel property (official, HockeyStack, 2026-09 validation; undated). Adapting that method to Threads is a measurement choice, not a validated Threads conversion mechanism (directional).

Chartbeat describes app and message traffic arriving without referrer information in its news-media sample (measured, Chartbeat, n=news-media network traffic, 2026-09 validation; undated). Keep the root self-contained for forwarding, retain optional source answers, and leave unknown origins unknown; these are GTM applications, not results measured by Chartbeat (directional).

Respondology reports off-hours commenting and associations between active moderation and less negative feedback across its sampled social comments (measured, Respondology, n=168.8 million comments; not a Threads-only sample, 2026-04). Set coverage from actual conversation activity and handle spam or impersonation through available controls. Don't import a moderation uplift into a Threads forecast (directional).

## Disclosure and voice evidence

For qualifying synthetic media, use Meta's disclosure tool: the requirement covers organic digitally created or altered photorealistic video and realistic-sounding audio. Meta also describes AI info labeling (official, Meta AI Info, 2026-02, updated 2026-06). Keep the distinction between a disclosure obligation, a viewer reaction, and a distribution decision. Don't generalize Instagram label experiments into Threads reach penalties.

The AI-tell guidance from Aborn, Cox, and Gichigi is practitioner editing advice (directional). It doesn't identify authorship. Lees's specificity and defended-decision advice is also practitioner guidance applied to Threads (directional). Preserve the author's source-backed stakes and punctuation; don't manufacture flaws.

MIDI found benefits from conversational context but continuing uneven idiom comprehension across language-resource levels (measured, MIDI, n=18-language benchmark, 2026-06). Fluent local review is an editorial inference (directional). The manuscript detector study found the same editing could move different detector scores in opposite directions (measured, Detector Study, n=135,389 manuscript pairs across 13 detectors, 2026-08). Review provenance and the actual text; don't use detector scores as a publishing gate (directional).
