## Purpose

Use this reference when deciding what to test, interpreting performance, or estimating economics. Observational findings describe selected samples; they don't establish a causal creative formula. Keep the measured result separate from your proposed action.

## Performance decisions

| Evidence | Writing or planning action |
|---|---|
| Long-form views rose while average duration and engagement fell (measured, Metricool statistics, n=799,718 videos from 71,177 accounts, 2026-07) | Inspect the upload's retention at the promised demonstration and its qualified responses; aggregate duration cannot diagnose an opening (directional) |
| Shorts views rose while duration and interactions fell; the reported average duration was roughly 16 seconds (measured, Metricool trends, n=799,718 videos, 2026-07) | Show value promptly without treating an average duration as a script deadline (directional) |
| Shorts supplied 61% of views in the broad report; a separate organic-source table attributed 61.54% to the Shorts feed (measured, Metricool press release and Shorts, n=799,718 videos, 2026-08) | Keep content-format share separate from traffic-source share; test a self-contained discovery asset and a deeper evaluation asset (directional) |
| Organic views also came through subscriber, search and related-video routes; hashtags accounted for 0.03% in the reported source table (measured, Metricool Shorts, n=799,718 videos, 2026-08) | Build useful search and follow-on viewing paths instead of treating hashtags as an acquisition strategy (directional) |
| Shorts views per post rose for smaller channels and fell for the largest cohort (measured, Metricool Shorts, n=799,718 videos, 2026-08) | Compare against similar channel sizes and upload ages; don't borrow a large channel's expected views (directional) |
| About five Shorts monthly correlated with higher long-form views; heavy Shorts output returned near the no-Shorts cohort (measured, Metricool Shorts, n=799,718 videos, 2026-08) | Test a modest bridge and observe the actual audience response; don't claim measured subscriber conversion or a ranking penalty (directional) |
| Per-Short results peaked at 2–4 weekly uploads; greater output had weaker per-post averages (measured, Metricool Shorts, n=799,718 videos, 2026-08) | Use capacity and quality to choose cadence; this is an observed cohort, not a posting requirement (directional) |
| Long-form uploads at 2–4 weekly averaged 10,552 views per post; moving to 4–7 yielded about 6% more monthly views (measured, Metricool trends, n=799,718 videos, 2026-07) | Evaluate incremental work against useful viewing and business outcomes before increasing production (directional) |
| Shorts with hashtags had about 10% fewer views and nearly 9% more engagement (measured, Metricool trends, n=799,718 videos, 2026-07) | Use relevant hashtags for context; don't infer that hashtags caused either change (directional) |
| At least 12 uploads monthly correlated with 4.1 times the median monthly view-growth rate of less-than-monthly channels (measured, vidIQ frequency, n=10,210,278 channels, 2026-07) | Treat volume as an observational planning input; resources and audience fit can confound the association (directional) |
| Witnessed-event and transformation titles had higher average views; question endings and confession titles were weaker in the corpus (measured, vidIQ titles, n=over 4 million uploads, 2026-07) | Name the demonstrable event or outcome; don't fabricate a transformation to fit a title formula. Cell counts and controls were unpublished (directional) |
| Natural faces were common; exaggerated expressions were uncommon (measured, vidIQ thumbnails, n=500 breakout thumbnails across 30 niches, 2026-07) | Choose a face only if it carries information; the sample doesn't prove a face causes success (directional) |
| 28% used no thumbnail text; text-bearing thumbnails had a five-word median (measured, vidIQ thumbnails, n=500 thumbnails, 2026-07) | Allow text-free thumbnails and reject a mandatory word quota (directional) |
| The best of the first ten uploads exceeded the first upload's views in selected persistent-channel cohorts (measured, vidIQ early uploads, n=448,413 combined, overlapping channel cohorts, 2026-07) | Plan early uploads as a portfolio of distinct hypotheses; don't demand linear improvement or predict a newcomer's outcome from a selected sample (directional) |
| In a 5,400-Short sample, Shorts under about 60% viewed-versus-swiped rarely performed, and the thread itself states that winners sat between 70 and 90% (measured, Galloway thread, n=5,400 Shorts, 2023-04; initial results) | Use it to sanity-check your own Studio comparison between similar Shorts; it isn't a target or a platform benchmark (directional) |
| The 11–20 second band had the highest median Shorts views (measured, quso.ai, n=about 508,000 posts in the full dataset; Shorts subset unstated, 2026-06) | Treat it as a cohort observation; finish the promise at its useful length (directional) |

## Revenue and packaging evidence

AIR found Shorts RPM from $0.02 to $1.48, generally 3–14% of comparable long-form RPM (measured, AIR, n=274 channels, 2026-06). Forecast from the channel's niche and Studio data. Don't publish a universal Shorts median, per-million payout, or blanket claim that Shorts cannot support a business (directional).

Galloway's interview summary supports investing effort in ideation and packaging, and recounts a thumbnail success story. Use packaging-first as craft guidance; don't present its time-allocation estimates or case multiplier as controlled measurements (directional; Colin and Samir).

## Myths to reject

Each row is a claim this evidence set does not support; use the stated fact instead (directional unless marked).

| Myth | What the evidence supports |
|---|---|
| Aim for 60–70% viewed on every Short | No official benchmark exists; compare viewed-versus-swiped with similar Shorts (official, YouTube Shorts analytics, checked 2026-09). |
| A voiceover over someone else's clip makes it original | YouTube drew no clear line; add commentary, a unique edit, or a story (official, YouTube Help community, 2026-10-01). |
| YouTube demotes TikTok watermarks | No official source says so. Export from a clean master as craft only. |
| Delete and re-upload a flopping Short | YouTube warned it risks a spam flag (official, Creator Insider with Todd Sherman, 2023-08-23). |
| Post Shorts at the optimal hour | YouTube said Shorts are not optimized for posting time (same source). |
| Shorts need hashtags | YouTube said they are not required (same source); the Metricool source table credits hashtags with 0.03% of organic views. |
| A higher view count after the count change means better content | Counting changed, not the algorithm; CTR, average view duration, and retention stay on engaged views (official, YouTube engaged views, 2026-08-19). |
