## Launch execution and evidence

Use the main skill's intake and illustrative calendar. Keep cross-platform sequencing outside this playbook. Evidence below supports testing an execution pattern; vendor cases aren't forecasts or policy permission.

### Build the brief around buying intent

Sprout reports product discovery and recurring engagement with brands on Instagram (measured, Sprout Instagram strategy, n=consumer survey; subset unstated, 2026-07). Define what a visitor is evaluating before choosing the format. Give the post a product path when useful; awareness alone needn't be the campaign's goal (directional).

Define the ideal follower and a manageable set of recurring subjects before scheduling; keep the account's promise consistent long enough to evaluate it (directional; Buffer Growth, 2026-08). Use the buyer's language for demonstrations and credible reviews. Instagram is among the leading social-search destinations in Sprout's survey (measured, Sprout Q2, n=2,000+ users in the US/UK/Australia, 2026-06); that doesn't establish exact caption-ranking mechanics or a superior channel mix.

Prepare approved assets for owned-channel references to the Instagram handle, including packaging, the website, email, and voluntary employee posts (directional; Buffer Growth, 2026-08). Record these as distribution dependencies; don't turn this into a cross-platform calendar or send outreach without authorization.

### Creator and employee participation

Emplifi found engagement lifts of 3.4 times for extra-small brands and 2.7 times for small brands in its Collab cohort (measured, Emplifi Collabs, n=4,823 brands, 2025-03). These replace unsupported universal impression multipliers. Confirm collaborator capacity in the composer instead of asserting a fixed co-author limit.

Relevant mentions were associated with greater reach and comments, especially for small accounts (measured, Metricool Trends, n=24,364,803 posts, 2026-06). Mention real contributors whose role the post explains; audience fit is a reason to collaborate, while audience overlap alone doesn't prove the collaboration is worthless (directional).

Give creators the business purpose, sourceable proof, and disclosure boundaries; let them choose an execution their audience recognizes. Audience-versus-brand tension appeared in CreatorIQ's survey (measured, CreatorIQ, n=5,095 creators, 2026-08). In Boostified's Oriflame case, gifted products and creative freedom accompanied an Instagram launch without cash compensation (measured, Boostified, n=single vendor-reported campaign, 2026-01). Neither source establishes a guaranteed lift or removes gift-disclosure obligations.

Consider voluntary employee demonstrations with transparent affiliation and agreed compensation. Sprout's survey records employee-driven discovery and consumer support for additional pay (measured, Sprout Q2, n=2,000+ users in the US/UK/Australia, 2026-06). This is a test idea, not evidence that paying employees produces Instagram sales (directional).

Supply assets and approved claims without scripted praise. Don't coordinate fabricated enthusiasm, engagement rings, or identical employee comments. Obtain permission to reuse creator or customer work; permission and recommendation eligibility are separate checks (directional).

### Resource delivery and DM qualification

Inro's Nona Source case used keyword-triggered DMs for private-sale registration; Manychat's Micha Menezes case combined organic validation with segmented comment-keyword flows and sales qualification (measured, Inro, n=single vendor-reported campaign, 2026-06; measured, Manychat Micha, n=single vendor-reported business, 2026-03). Use these as examples of possible workflows, not blanket approval or expected conversion rates.

State the resource and what commenting requests. Deliver it promptly through a tested flow, then offer optional qualification and a human handoff. Don't simulate a personal voice note, manufacture a typing delay, or imply that an automated message was individually written. Match the author's regional register without fabricating intimacy (directional).

Validate the organic post and complete delivery flow before considering paid amplification. Track requested resources, successful deliveries, qualified conversations, and registrations separately. Only contact people commercially with consent; an existing relationship or contextual guess isn't permission for repeated messages (directional).

Metricool associated questions with 37% more comments, comment requests with 203% more comments, save requests with 92% more saves, and like requests with 4.9% fewer likes (measured, Metricool Trends, n=24,364,803 posts, 2026-06). These are correlations. Ask an answerable question when its answer matters; use a resource CTA when delivery is the intended action. Don't optimize for comment totals without evaluating downstream quality (directional).

### Sequence proof after the reveal

Boomerang's ZORÁE case moved from Story unboxings with registration links to launch coverage, then lifestyle Reels. Creator-specific Story links supported attribution; post-event use-in-context Reels had the campaign's highest engagement rates (measured, Boomerang, n=single agency-reported campaign, 2026-08). Adapt this pattern when the product benefits from seeing actual use; don't copy the agency's results as a forecast (directional).

Plan the customer's ordinary use after announcement day. A recap can address an unresolved objection, show a limitation, or explain a changed decision. Use actual source material and retain the conditions around any result. Reserve founder first-person claims for decisions or experience the founder supplied (directional).

Metricool measured 70% of lifetime views arriving in the first three days; Reels received 65% of views in that period, compared with 76% for posts/carousels (measured, Metricool Trends, n=24,364,803 posts, 2026-06). Schedule follow-up capacity while work is fresh; don't declare older posts dead or claim Story redistribution causes a rescue (directional).

Story replies grew 88% year over year in the study (measured, Metricool study, n=24,364,803 posts/375,118 accounts, 2026-06). This supports testing a conversation path; it isn't a Story completion or link-conversion benchmark. Put the context in the opening frame, test available interactive stickers, and send requested private details through consented DMs (directional).

### Staff the conversation

Creator replies correlated with about 21% higher engagement in a fixed-effects Instagram analysis (measured, Buffer Replies, n=700,000+ posts/nearly 68,000 accounts, 2026-01). Assign a reply owner as part of the package without promising an algorithmic reward (directional).

Respondology reported substantial comment activity outside office hours and widespread spam or abuse; consumer perceptions of brands were associated with comment quality (measured, Respondology, n=168.8M comments plus consumer research, 2026-04). Arrange evening or weekend coverage when the chosen publishing time needs it, remove fraudulent links, and distinguish criticism from abuse. Escalate service problems without soliciting private customer data publicly (directional).

Manychat found low expectations of receiving creator replies (measured, Manychat Report, n=1,028 daily social-media consumers, undated, reviewed 2026-09). Reserve personal responses for substantive questions and relationship building; automation can deliver resources without impersonating a human (directional).

### Diagnose outcomes before increasing volume

Buffer's frequency findings differ by cohort: its Instagram-specific analysis associated greater frequency with higher per-post reach, while its cross-platform report found reduced per-post reach at higher frequency and a narrower top-performer cadence gap on Instagram than on text-led networks (measured, Buffer Frequency, n=2.1M Instagram posts/102K accounts, 2025-08; measured, Buffer State, n=4.8M channel-weeks and 15.7M-post reach analysis, 2026-03). Use sustainable, production-aware experiments; don't merge those scopes into a universal frequency rule (directional).

Name the denominator and attribution window in every report. Treat platform engagement as a within-platform benchmark; use consistently defined registrations, activation, bookings, or revenue for business outcomes (directional; Buffer State, 2026-03). Match the CTA to the available measurement field; don't call profile taps customers.

Use distinct tagged links for creator and placement attribution, and verify the destination through signup or checkout. Test loading and form behavior inside Instagram. EscapeHatch reported better downstream results with system-browser handoff in an anonymous fragrance-store experiment (measured, EscapeHatch, n=53,784 randomized visitors; first-party report, undated, reviewed 2026-09). This supports testing browser friction, not requiring a handoff or promising the same lift (directional).

Don't assign blended launch-day outcomes entirely to Instagram. A founder's OIO launch report pooled signups across several channels and attributed its paying customer elsewhere (measured, OIO founder report, n=single unaudited launch, 2026-09). Preserve unknown sources rather than forcing attribution (directional).
