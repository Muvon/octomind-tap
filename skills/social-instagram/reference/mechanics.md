### Reels and changing surfaces

Use the measured narrative band in the main skill as a starting test, then inspect the account's actual retention and task completion. Don't enforce the former unsupported average-watch-time budget. Don't turn replay-inclusive total watch time into a guaranteed ranking comparison.

Meta announced Trial Reels available to everyone and sharing to non-followers first (official, Meta Creativity, 2025-06). Check for the control on the publishing account. Use it for exploratory hook testing, record audience and timing differences, and don't describe it as randomized A/B testing. No verified follower gate, exclusive audience guarantee, fixed insights window, grid behavior, or auto-sharing threshold is established here.

Series was testing with selected creators as an ordered profile hub for existing or new Reels that viewers can save and resume (reported platform guidance, Meta via TechCrunch Series, 2026-06). When available, package an episodic tutorial with independent entry points; otherwise make each Reel self-contained (directional).

Instagram expanded television availability to Samsung TVs in the US and enabled Reel casting on Google TV and Fire TV; Stories and a horizontal-video home remained tests (official, Meta TV, 2026-06). For likely communal viewing, check text at viewing distance and preserve meaning outside small overlays; don't impose horizontal delivery on ordinary Reels (directional).

Your Algorithm controls were testing additional Feed and Reels entry points and more-or-less preferences; some tests might not ship (reported platform guidance, Mosseri via TechCrunch Controls, 2026-06). Don't promise that a format guarantees distribution, or infer a niche-coherence ranking rule from this announcement.

First Draft was announced as a starting cut assembled from selected clips, with provisional availability details (reported platform guidance, Instagram via NapoleonCat, 2026-08). If present, use it for assembly and inspect the opening, sequence, subtitle accuracy, and author voice before publication (directional).

### Audio and publishing handoff

Replace Audio allows changing music on published feed posts and carousels while retaining likes, comments, shares, and reach (reported platform guidance, Instagram via TechCrunch Audio, 2026-07). Check that control before deleting a post solely to repair its soundtrack; don't extend this scope to every Reel (directional).

Facebook Login apps can search original Reel audio and Sound Collection music, retrieve metadata, attach audio during Reel creation, and find royalty-free ad replacements (reported platform guidance, Meta developer changelog via Releasebot, 2026-06). Supply the chosen audio identifier and required usage scope to downstream publishing. Confirm the scheduler supports that path; don't assume all catalog tracks are commercially cleared (directional).

Record the actual audio-page URL reached from the Reel and the displayed title or usage count when available. Compare observations over time; don't infer an algorithm boost from audio-page presence. Trending music can fit a scene without proving rights or improving its performance (directional).

### Search, tags, and measurement limits

Hashtag following ended in December 2024, and the verified report describes the five-hashtag cap (reported platform guidance, Instagram via Metricool Trends, 2026-06). Hashtagged posts had about 31% fewer views and 33% fewer interactions, correlationally (measured, Metricool, n=24,364,803 posts, 2026-06). Use literal classification tags when useful; don't infer a hashtag penalty or repeat an unsupported Mosseri quotation.

Write product nouns and use-case language that an interested visitor recognizes. Social search and product-review intent support useful explanations, not keyword stuffing. Google indexing eligibility, exact caption-search matching, on-screen-text matching, and caption-link rollout aren't established by this evidence set. Check live account controls before promising those paths (directional).

Retain metric names exactly as provided by the account export. Distinguish views, reach, and actions; calculate a rate only with a supplied numerator and denominator. Don't declare impressions or plays universally extinct, or rename aggregate shares as private sends (directional).

Story link taps are available as `link_clicks` through the Facebook Login version of the Media Insights API; the source gives inconsistent introduction dates (reported platform guidance, Meta developer changelog via Releasebot, 2026-06 report). Ask downstream reporting to confirm the authentication path and field availability; retain in-product reporting when the API field isn't available (directional).

### AI disclosure and audience response

Meta describes “AI info” and requires disclosure for organic digitally created or altered photorealistic video and realistic-sounding audio (official, Meta Midterms, 2026-06 update). Use the native disclosure tool when applicable. Don't infer label placement, metadata triggers, or a blanket text-assistance requirement from this announcement.

The publishing API accepts `is_ai_generated=true`; for a carousel it belongs only on the parent container (reported platform guidance, Meta developer changelog via Releasebot, 2026-06). Include the disclosure instruction in a scheduler handoff when applicable; the field's existence doesn't require every post to use it.

Ads created or significantly edited using Meta tools receive labels, and Meta is beginning detection of third-party AI signals (official, Meta Ads, 2026-06 update). This source doesn't establish blanket advertiser self-disclosure for every AI-assisted ad. Confirm applicable advertising requirements separately.

Instagram's “AI-generated profile” label applies to profiles centered on a synthetic person; undisclosed profiles face reach reduction, while a properly disclosed synthetic identity isn't penalized merely for being synthetic (reported platform guidance, Instagram via TechCrunch Profiles, 2026-08). Keep that profile rule separate from media labels.

Label experiments found lower self-reported engagement, particularly for emotional visual content (measured, Electronic Markets, n=325 and n=371 participants, 2026-03). Another experiment found reduced authenticity ratings for labeled synthetic images and a smaller increase for unlabeled images (measured, ICWSM, n=877 German participants, 2026-05). Keep required disclosure and test audience interpretation; neither study measures Instagram distribution, text-only captions, or sales. Missing labels don't establish authentic provenance.

Use editorial checks and source records instead of detector scores as publication gates. Human editing moved detector scores in conflicting directions in academic manuscripts (measured, Editing preprint, n=135,389 manuscript pairs, 2026-08); it doesn't supply a social-post error rate. Retain real tradeoffs in stories: tidy-story research concerns fiction, so its application to Instagram is an inference (directional; StoryScope, 2026-08). Have a fluent editor review localized idioms in context (directional; MIDI, 2026-06).

### Commercial copy and accessible meaning

Instagram's branded-content definition includes payment, gifts, and loaned products as exchanges of value requiring its paid-partnership label (official, Branded Content Help, indexed text checked 2026-09; full page requires account access). Identify the actual relationship in the copy too; tagging a collaborator doesn't communicate that relationship by itself. Do not generalize this Instagram control into a Threads requirement.

Write alt text from the actual image: identify the meaningful object, action, comparison, and essential embedded wording without inventing an emotion or repeating a promotional caption. For a chart or proof export, make the result and its conditions available in readable text; “a chart” is not an equivalent description. Check generated descriptions rather than assuming object recognition captures the point (directional; Meta Accessibility). Automatic description, faithful evidence, and authorship are separate questions.
