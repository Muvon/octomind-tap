## Overview

Open this reference when preparing creator briefs, Shop/LIVE, Spark Ads, paid amplification, or launch measurement. Use only the branches requested by the campaign brief; paid operations and publication remain downstream steps. All editorial recommendations are (directional) unless their mechanism has an explicit source label.

## Build the creator brief

Give the creator the buying situation, source-backed promise, approved evidence and limitations, desired action, destination, disclosure obligations, usage rights, and prohibited claims. Agree who reviews factual accuracy and who owns replies. Leave delivery and narrative choices to the creator within those boundaries (directional).

Separate words that must remain accurate from suggested delivery. Preserve the creator's own reaction; don't supply “I use this every day” unless they do. An excerpt must retain the qualification that changes its meaning, even when a shorter cut would fit another platform. Give each creator room to choose a real observation rather than assigning identical hooks and comment scripts (directional).

CreatorIQ reports tension between audience preferences and brand requests (measured, CreatorIQ, n=5,095 surveyed creators, 2026-08). Preserve the creator's documented voice instead of supplying an identical script to everyone (directional).

Employee content featured in reported product discovery, and respondents supported extra compensation for employee influencers (measured, Sprout, n=over 2,000 social users in the US/UK/Australia, 2026-06). Invite willing employees to demonstrate work they know and agree compensation; the survey doesn't establish conversion lift (directional).

TikTok was among the leading reported social-search destinations, with product reviews a reported search preference (measured, Sprout, n=over 2,000 social users in the US/UK/Australia, 2026-06). Make demonstrations answer a buying question and show the conditions of a review. Validate audience demand before promising a search-led launch (directional).

TikTok One Content Suite provides AI Search, Projects and Creators for finding relevant UGC and managing creator relationships (official, Content Suite announcement, 2026-06). Shortlist people already discussing the product; preserve their original contribution and document permission before reuse (directional).

## Publish and evaluate within TikTok

Use the launch-week table in the main skill as an adjustable editorial schedule. Draft real answers to likely evaluation questions and record unresolved objections for a later demonstration. Reuse a topic only when the next post adds evidence (directional).

Track the native metrics actually available to the account: retention/watch behavior for comprehension, saves and relevant shares for usefulness, substantive comments for buying context, and profile/product actions for the next step. Declare the engagement-rate denominator and observation window; compare like definitions within TikTok. Separate paid delivery from organic results and connect visits to qualified inquiries, retained signups or purchases downstream (directional).

Use consistent source, medium and campaign parameters; use utm_content to distinguish creative/link variants (official, Google Analytics URL guidance, undated; verified 2026-09). Verify the actual destination works and preserves approved attribution before publication (directional).

Plan reply and moderation coverage when viewers are active, including evenings or weekends when warranted. Cross-platform comment research reports substantial activity outside office hours and spam/abuse in comments (measured, Respondology, n=168.8M comments, 2026-04). A consumer survey found low expectations of creator replies (measured, Manychat, n=1,028 daily social-media consumers, undated; verified 2026-09). Answer personally where useful, remove fraudulent links, and escalate customer-specific matters privately with consent; don't automate a fake personal exchange (directional). Neither study supplies a TikTok reply reach multiplier.

Don't organize bought engagement, fake reviews, engagement trades or repetitive irrelevant seeding; TikTok's rules address these practices with account, search or For You restrictions (official, Integrity and Authenticity, 2025-08). Relevant employee and creator participation should disclose its relationship and add its own context (directional).
