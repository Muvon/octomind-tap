## Overview

Open this reference when quoting benchmarks, explaining recommendation or AI mechanisms, reviewing production constraints, or advising a creator about earnings. Keep numbers in their source populations. A measured association is a test input, not a promised result.

## Benchmarks and decisions

| Evidence | What to do with it |
|---|---|
| Video volume rose 72.10% year over year and image/carousel volume nearly 140%; video views fell 31.30%, reach 28.73%, and interactions 31.17% (measured, Metricool, n=2,314,756 posts from over 92,000 accounts, 2026-05). | Compare performance with account history and matched peers; don't treat more output as sufficient. |
| For You supplied 72.70% of video views (measured, Metricool, n=2,314,756 posts, 2026-05). | Give unfamiliar viewers enough context to understand the proof (directional). |
| Questions correlated with 26.19% more comments; requests for comments with 14% higher engagement; requests for likes with 60% fewer interactions (measured, Metricool, n=2,314,756 posts, 2026-05). | Ask only an answerable question that serves the discussion. Don't extrapolate the like-request result to follows or claim causality. |
| Hashtag-bearing posts had nearly 5% more views and over 9% more interactions; hashtag traffic grew 114% year over year (measured, Metricool, n=2,314,756 posts, 2026-05). | Use descriptive tags without prescribing an optimal count. |
| Video produced five times the reach and six times the interactions of images/carousels in the press release (measured, Metricool, n=2,314,756 posts, 2026-05). | Start with video for demonstrations. The study landing page calls the first comparison views instead; don't silently interchange reach and views or merge these differently worded claims. |
| Video median engagement was 3.39%, versus 1.92% for photos/carousels (measured, Buffer Formats, n=45M+ cross-platform posts, 2026-03). | Use Buffer's within-study comparison; don't describe its full corpus as TikTok-only. Preserve the source's engagement definition and flag it as unspecified when unavailable. |
| Short videos of 15–30 seconds led engagement at 6.00%; videos of 120–180 seconds led median views at 11,136 (measured, Socialinsider, n=6M brand videos posted January–June, 2026-07). | Choose the goal before choosing length; neither result establishes conversion or search superiority. |
| Posts accumulated 96% of reach and nearly 98% of interactions within the first 10 days (measured, Metricool, n=2,314,756 posts, 2026-05). | Use the window for a review, never a trend expiration or instruction to delete old posts. |
| Evenings from 6–11 p.m. performed broadly well, Saturday led by day, and Sunday at 9 a.m. led individual slots; no universal best time exists (measured, Buffer Timing, n=7.1M posts, 2026-07). | Test against audience-local analytics; don't impose a global clock. |
| Higher frequency was associated with better breakout odds while median views stayed flat (measured, Buffer Frequency, n=TikTok posting-frequency analysis; sample not established in cited guide, 2026-01). | Raise cadence only when fresh evidence and reply capacity permit; don't promise an improved typical post. |

Buffer's top-performer cadence gap was narrower on TikTok than on text-led networks, with resources and momentum unresolved as explanations (measured, Buffer Engagement, n=52M+ cross-platform posts, 2026-03). Budget production time before promising output. The same report's reply-lift analysis excluded TikTok; don't attach a TikTok reply multiplier.

Formats performed differently across Instagram and TikTok in Metricool's comparison (measured, Metricool Instagram study, n=24,364,803 Instagram posts with TikTok comparison, 2026-06). Recut the hook and CTA from clean assets for TikTok as an editorial choice (directional); don't transplant another network's carousel strategy or compare incompatible engagement rates.

Reported TikTok daily-use depth came from Android usage, not viewing time for a particular brand or post (measured, The Star usage report, n=February Android usage summary, 2026-08). Test related episodes only when the audience has continuing questions (directional); time spent on the app doesn't establish demand for your series.

## Historical mechanisms and production

The recommendation explainer lists interactions, video information and device/account settings, describes completion of a longer video as stronger than shared geography, and says consecutive recommendations generally avoid the same creator or sound (official, TikTok recommendation explainer, 2020-06). These are historical descriptions, not verified current model invariants. Don't infer a universal follower-first test, fixed initial pool, current numeric weights, or saturation penalty from them.

The playbook calls the opening 3–6 seconds critical and recommends a hook followed by selling points and a CTA (official, SMB Playbook, undated; verified 2026-09). Treat the structure as a creative scaffold; it isn't an organic ranking threshold.

Older advertising studies in the playbook report 33% stronger consideration for lo-fi ads, 75% positive response to TikTok-first creative, and 24% higher favorability for creator ads (measured, TikTok/Lumen/Ipsos/Hotspex, n=ad-study populations; sample counts not supplied, studies 2021–2023, compiled playbook verified 2026-09). These support testing understandable creator-native production; they don't prove that every handheld video beats studio footage. Remove unsupported heavy-user and muted-viewing percentages.

The production recommendation is full-screen 9:16, no lower than 720p, and sound (official, SMB Playbook, undated; verified 2026-09). Clear speech, subtitles and readable evidence remain craft requirements (directional). The Commercial Music Library supplies tracks cleared for paid advertising and the playbook describes an approved-for-business-use filter (official, SMB Playbook, undated; verified 2026-09). Check the selected track and live filter wording; don't assume the same rights for every territory, placement or export. No verified audio-analysis claim supports a silent trending-sound tactic.

## Earnings and search

Ask whether the author is actually pursuing Creator Rewards. Obtain current account and regional terms before giving eligibility, duration or qualified-view advice. The retained sources do not establish current follower/view thresholds, the duration floor, qualified-view seconds or exclusions; don't recycle them as current facts.

TikTok's historical announcement named originality, play duration, search value and audience engagement as program priorities (official, Creator format announcement, 2024-03). That isn't a current RPM equation. Don't promise higher earnings from searchable copy or state a current program formula without re-verifying it.

Use buyer vocabulary in speech and captions for clarity (directional). The evidence does not verify organic spoken-keyword indexing, current Keyword Insights search-demand coverage, or the old Adobe survey attribution. Leave those claims out. Confirm the live search surface before promising a specific search workflow.

## AI and regional scope

TikTok reported over 3 billion AIGC-labeled videos, creator labels, Content Credentials and invisible watermarking; it also reported joining the C2PA Steering Committee (official, TikTok AI Update, 2026-07). Retain provenance metadata and don't infer a launch date for watermarking from this announcement.

The same announcement describes Manage Topics AIGC controls and testing detection of accounts dedicated to AI spam in political/current-event, financial-advice and medical content (official, TikTok AI Update, 2026-07). Separate the test announcement from deployed global enforcement. Review factual claims and original contribution in sensitive-topic assets (directional). Re-verify availability and regional policy before citing these mechanisms.

For US Shop, fully generated or significantly AI-edited content requires disclosure, fabricated or exaggerated AI product effects are prohibited, and the label alone does not demote compliant content (official, US Shop AIGC Rules, 2026-07). Keep that assurance inside its US Shop scope. Audience reaction, recommendation eligibility and earnings are different questions. Don't claim a universal AI reach effect or use a viral anecdote to prove one.

Symphony Creative Studio is available to logged-in TikTok for Business marketers and automatically labels its generated videos (official, Symphony announcement, 2026-06). Review generated demonstrations against the real product; a label doesn't make invented proof usable.

Don't generalize US Shop, UK case studies or the New Zealand newsroom announcement into identical global/US operating rules. Obtain applicable market guidance for the finished asset, including commercial and synthetic-media disclosures. No verified EU-law or ownership-specific algorithm conclusion is supplied here.
