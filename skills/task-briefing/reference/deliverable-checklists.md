# Deliverable checklists

Outcome-changing slots per deliverable form. Fill each slot from the request, then context, then a recorded default. Ask only for slots in the "user-only" column, and only when the readings diverge. A slot missing from a row is the executor's craft call: leave it out of the brief.

Use the closest row. A request that spans two rows (a launch post plus a reply plan) gets one brief per deliverable.

## Writing and content

| Deliverable | Outcome-changing slots | Safe default when unstated | User-only (ask if divergent) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Social post | Platform, goal (awareness, clicks, replies, signups), audience, the one point or offer, link or CTA, voice | Voice from existing posts or brand docs; one post, not a thread | Platform when none is implied; the claim or offer itself; whether it publishes or only drafts |
| Article or blog post | Topic and angle, reader and their level, purpose (rank, educate, convert), length band, sources the piece must use | Length from the venue's existing posts; neutral expert voice | Angle when two differ in substance; claims about the user's own product or results |
| Email or message | Recipient and relationship, the single ask, desired outcome, tone, deadline or date involved | Short; one ask; polite-direct tone | Recipient; the ask; any commitment, price, or date offered |
| Reply to a comment or review | Thread or text being answered, stance (thank, correct, resolve, decline), what can be offered | Public, calm, no admission beyond the facts given | Any refund, discount, or admission |
| Presentation or talk | Audience, decision or takeaway, duration or slide budget, setting (live, async, pitch) | Ten slides for a twenty-minute talk | Audience and the takeaway |
| Translation or localization | Target language and locale, register, glossary or terms to keep, length limits | Keep product names and code untranslated | Locale variant when it changes wording (pt-BR or pt-PT) |
| Edit or rewrite | What to keep (voice, facts, length), what to fix, how far to go (proofread, line edit, restructure) | Preserve facts and voice; line edit | Depth when the text is long or public |

## Marketing, sales, launch

| Deliverable | Outcome-changing slots | Safe default when unstated | User-only (ask if divergent) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Ad or landing copy | Platform and placement, product, audience and awareness stage, offer, CTA, character limits | Problem-aware stage; one variant set of three | Offer, price, any guarantee |
| Outreach sequence | Channel, persona and company type, trigger or signal, the ask, number of touches | Three touches over ten days | Persona; claims about results or customers |
| Campaign or launch plan | Goal and metric, budget band, channels already owned, date, audience | Owned channels first; zero paid spend | Budget; launch date |
| Video script or ad | Product, audience, platform, length, awareness stage, offer and CTA | Platform-native length (fifteen to sixty seconds short-form) | Offer; whether a real person appears on camera |

## Research, data, decisions

| Deliverable | Outcome-changing slots | Safe default when unstated | User-only (ask if divergent) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Research or fact-finding | The question, the decision it feeds, scope (time window, region, sources), depth, output shape | Last twelve months; primary sources preferred; cited summary | The decision the research serves, when the topic is broad |
| Comparison or recommendation | Options, criteria and their weight, hard constraints, budget | Criteria inferred from the stated goal, listed as assumptions | Budget; must-have constraints |
| Data analysis | The business question, data source and grain, time range, metric definitions, output (number, chart, table) | Most recent complete period | The decision behind the number |
| Spreadsheet or formula | App and version, input layout, expected output, edge cases (blanks, duplicates) | Current Microsoft 365 or Google Sheets functions | App when it gates which functions exist |
| SQL query | Dialect and version, tables, what each row returns, filters | Read-only query | Whether it may write or delete |

## Personal and everyday

| Deliverable | Outcome-changing slots | Safe default when unstated | User-only (ask if divergent) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Trip plan | Dates or length, origin, travellers, budget band, pace, must-sees | Moderate pace; mid-range budget | Dates; budget; who travels |
| Purchase advice | Use case, budget, where they buy (country), must-haves, what they own already | Current models available in the user's region | Budget; country |
| Meal or nutrition plan | People and portions, diet limits and allergies, time per meal, equipment | Thirty minutes per meal; common equipment | Allergies; medical diet |
| Learning or habit plan | Goal and level, time per week, deadline or exam date, preferred format | Three sessions per week | Current level; deadline |
| Legal, health, or money question | Jurisdiction or country, the person's role, the decision or deadline, documents at hand | None: these slots change the answer | Jurisdiction; deadlines; personal figures |

## Building and operating

| Deliverable | Outcome-changing slots | Safe default when unstated | User-only (ask if divergent) |
|---|---|---|---|
| Software change | Observable behavior wanted, who it affects, what must not change, how done is proven | Existing project conventions | Scope when two features fit the words; data deletion or access rules |
| Website or app task in a browser | URL, fields or actions, login needed, output shape | One page; JSON output | Credentials; anything submitted or bought |
| Design or UX review | Screens or URL, user and task, standard (WCAG level), output (issues list, redesign) | WCAG 2.2 AA; ranked issues list | Which flow when several exist |
| Operations or incident | System, symptom and since when, what changed, allowed actions | Read-only diagnosis first | Any restart, rollback, or deletion |

## Slots every brief checks

- Irreversible or outward step (publish, send, pay, delete, submit, book): the brief says "draft only" or "requires explicit approval before executing", never pre-approves.
- Language of the deliverable: the user's language unless stated.
- Deadline or date that changes the content.
- Regulated subject (health, law, money, safety): jurisdiction and the person's situation, plus "information, not professional advice".
