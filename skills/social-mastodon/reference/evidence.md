## Use evidence to choose experiments

Separate observed associations from decisions you want to test. Keep the source population and observation period attached to every finding; don't turn a study of crisis discussion or academic migration into a product-launch forecast.

### Format and timing

Buffer reports a median of about 3 interactions per Mastodon post through 2025, without meaningful year-over-year change. Images and video each had median 3; links and text each had median 2. The report doesn't disclose its Mastodon subsample and publishes no Mastodon-specific timing window (measured, Buffer, n=Mastodon subsample undisclosed, 2026-03).

Test accessible media when it shows the product or supports the claim. The observed difference is modest and non-causal. Equal link/text medians don't prove the absence of a link penalty. Don't compare interaction counts to another platform's reach-based rate, derive a posting quota, or import another platform's best-time table (directional).

### Conversation, tags, and cross-server participation

The ICWSM discussion study examines Gaza-related discourse collected in 2023. Its descriptive comparison uses 54,333 seed posts; the full corpus contains 97,687 posts (measured, Discussion study, n=54,333 seeds/97,687 total posts, 2026-05). Keep that population explicit.

| Observation | Decision and limit |
|---|---|
| Externally replied posts had median 41 words versus 30 for non-replied posts (measured, Discussion study, n=54,333 seeds, 2026-05). | Give a substantive thought enough context; don't impose a universal optimal length. |
| Externally replied posts had median 3 tags versus 4 in the other groups (measured, Discussion study, n=54,333 seeds, 2026-05). | Test relevant tags, not maximum tag count; this supplies no follower-size threshold or hard tag cap. |
| Externally replied cascades had median size 3 versus 2 for self-replied cascades, with greater breadth and cross-server diversity (measured, Discussion study, n=54,333 seeds, 2026-05). | Review independent participation separately from the author's installments; causality remains unproven. |
| Self-replies accounted for 26.23% of replies; self-replied cascades had median zero cross-server replies (measured, Discussion study, n=97,687 posts, 2026-05). | Add self-replies to explain something necessary; don't assume chain depth generates discovery. |
| Mention counts were the strongest classifier predictors, and URL count was influential; error analysis found overestimation of mention-heavy and link-heavy posts (measured, Discussion study, n=54,333 seeds, 2026-05). | Cite real sources and involved collaborators. Don't add mentions or links merely to imitate predictors. |
| Server betweenness had the largest network-variable effect magnitudes; large hubs and some small bridge servers connected communities (measured, Discussion study, n=54,333 seeds, 2026-05). | Inspect actual cross-server participants instead of equating the largest server with the best audience. |
| Externally replied authors had more followers, followed more accounts, and posted more often (measured, Discussion study, n=54,333 seeds, 2026-05). | Compare account context before crediting copy; don't infer that mass-following or extra posting causes replies. |

### Community continuity and recurring work

A study of academic migrants found topic-specific servers and profile discoverability associated with lower inactivity. Early cross-server interaction diversity was also associated with lower inactivity (measured, Academic migration study, n=7,357 accounts, 2026-05). Explore audience fit and useful participation as hypotheses. The study doesn't establish commercial conversion effects or justify moving a functioning account for reach.

A maintainer's recurring Rust-project series reported 786 Mastodon posts from December 15, 2022 through March 31, 2026, with 10,655 favourites, 4,529 boosts, and 658 replies; it later became a Top 99 resource (measured, Orhun self-report, n=786 posts, 2026-04). This is an account-specific case, not a platform benchmark. Retain sources and artifacts from a useful recurring series so they can support an owned directory or recap (directional). Don't borrow the maintainer's frequency as a quota or present the totals as attainable forecasts.

### Launch links into other communities

A Mastodon announcement linking to another community must honor the destination's rules. Product Hunt permits feedback requests but prohibits upvote requests and vote rewards (official, Product Hunt sharing, undated; reviewed 2026-09). Show HN requires a usable artifact and maker availability, excludes signup-page-only submissions, and forbids asking friends to upvote or comment (official, Show HN, undated; reviewed 2026-09). These are destination-specific rules, not Mastodon ranking mechanisms. Keep any linked invitation compliant without copying a coordinated-voting campaign into Mastodon.

### Voice evidence and its limits

Aborn flags gratuitous contrast framing and habitual emphasis words, while rejecting deliberate typos as proof of human origin. Cox flags padded triads and excessive em-dashes while retaining useful punctuation. Gichigi flags repeated staccato cadence, suspense pivots, and compulsory summary endings. Apply these as editing checks, with no detector claim or numerical punctuation threshold (directional).

StoryScope reports more explicit themes and tidier plots in AI fiction (measured, StoryScope, n=61,608 stories, 2026-08). Applying that to a product story is an inference: inspect whether a neat transformation has erased costs or unresolved problems (directional).

Lees's advice ties specificity to real decisions and their costs. Apply it to sourced author notes, not invented autobiographical detail (directional). MIDI finds conversational context helps idiom comprehension while disparities remain across languages (measured, MIDI, n=18 languages, 2026-06). Have a fluent local editor review idiom when adapting a post; this is an editorial inference, not a measured engagement benefit (directional).

A manuscript-editing preprint found that the same professional edits moved different AI detectors in opposing directions (measured, Editing study, n=135,389 manuscript pairs, 2026-08). Don't use detector scores to certify short social posts. Check the author's source material and finished meaning (directional).

No cited study here measures a Mastodon-wide AI-label reach effect. Keep disclosure obligations separate from craft evaluation and reader-response hypotheses.
