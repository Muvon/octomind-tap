## Overview

Use this reference when a format choice depends on benchmark evidence or a brief depends on current LinkedIn controls. Keep observations distinct from editorial recommendations. Vendor cohorts differ in account selection, denominators, and comparison periods; none supplies a universal reach formula.

## Platform mechanics and feature checks

LinkedIn describes retrieval using language-model representations and sequential ranking informed by profile data and interaction history, including reads and revisits. It doesn't publish a universal ordering of signals or comment-to-reaction weights (official, LinkedIn Feed Engineering, 2026-03). Don't infer dwell time from paragraph count, or distribution causality from public reactions (directional).

Posts have a 3,000-character limit and Articles are a separate destination for longer work (official, LinkedIn Post Help, 2026-08). Preview the completed asset in the target account. Treat mobile and desktop truncation estimates as interface observations, not hard limits; no fixed visible-character budget belongs in the checklist (directional; AuthoredUp Length, 2026-08).

Creator Mode's toggle was removed in March 2024 (official, LinkedIn Creator Mode Help, 2024-03). Don't replace the retired feature with an invented Premium, verification, or Top Voice reach boost. Verified-member filters now cover profile views, applications, comments, and feed conversations (official, LinkedIn Authenticity, 2026-06). Check eligible identity/workplace verification during account preparation; a badge doesn't validate a post's claims (directional).

LinkedIn's personal AI writing tool is currently unavailable, with replacement tooling planned; the source doesn't establish a retirement date (official, LinkedIn Writing Tool Help, 2026-08). Keep the writing workflow independent of that control.

For a Newsletter brief, define a recurring subject, intended reader, editorial promise, and issue plan. Confirm account access and current subscription controls before promising a delivery mechanism. For an Article, prepare a sourced argument with useful headings and a feed introduction; turn reader objections into revisions or follow-up posts (directional). LinkedIn recommends an anchor-article and supporting-post feedback loop (official, LinkedIn AI Visibility, 2026-06); treat its suggested cadence as editorial guidance, not a ranking threshold.

For Live, create a scheduled Event before broadcasting; scheduling is required from June 22, 2026, including short-notice broadcasts (official, LinkedIn Live Help, 2026-06). Prepare the event title, explicit audience timezone, discussion promise, host responsibilities, and an accessible replay brief (directional). For Audio Events, check current account controls before drafting an invitation that promises the standalone format. Don't infer availability, cancellation, or a current name from old playbooks.

## Format evidence and writing decisions

### Visual formats and polls

Socialinsider's table labeled Q2 2026 reports engagement of 6.90% for multi-image, 6.60% for native documents, 5.90% for video, 5.20% for single images, 4.50% for polls, 3.95% for text, and 3.30% for links (measured, Socialinsider, n=1.3M business posts, 2026-03). Its methodology still says collection ended in December 2025 (measured, Socialinsider, n=1.3M business posts, 2026-03). Retain this date conflict whenever citing the table; don't present it as a clean current-period benchmark. Use the findings to include multi-image and documents in testing, and stop calling polls dead (directional).

Metricool reports average impressions of 1,451.19 for carousels versus 605.57 for video, and engagement rates of 3.71% for multi-image, 1.80% for video, and 1.81% for single images (measured, Metricool, n=673,658 posts from 63,108 accounts, 2026-04). Its Page analysis found polls had the highest average impressions despite being uncommon (measured, Metricool Press, n=673,658-post study, 2026-04). Test a decision-relevant poll or illustrated procedure when suitable; don't fill the calendar automatically with video (directional).

AuthoredUp reports document reach/engagement at 1.39×/1.30×, image at 1.20×/1.33×, video at 0.86×/0.93×, and reshares at 0.29×/0.22× relative to each profile's median (measured, AuthoredUp Formats, n=3M+ personal-profile posts, 2026-09). This doesn't establish a special commentary-repost ranking bonus. Add original interpretation and preserve the original source when reposting (directional).

Buffer reports carousel median engagement at 21.77%, versus video 7.35%, image 6.52%, links 3.81%, and text 3.18% in its predominantly prior-year data (measured, Buffer State, n=52M+ posts across the report; LinkedIn-format subset unspecified, 2026-03). These aren't interchangeable with other vendors' rates. Use documents for explanations that benefit from sequential inspection, rather than claiming the rate is a target (directional).

For PDF documents, make the cover state the reader's task; arrange slides in the order the task requires. Use readable type and an actual diagram or evidence where useful; end with the next usable step, rather than a compulsory save/follow request. Supply accessible text, source captions, and a plain-text summary. Don't impose an optimal slide count (directional).

LinkedIn may generate alt text for images without it; mobile uploaders may receive no alert. Open and review each description for the actual chart, screenshot labels, or comparison being discussed. Automatic descriptions don't establish factual accuracy or replace a document's accessible text (official, Alt Text Help, reviewed 2026-09-05; review procedure is editorial).

### Image and video production

Portrait single images had 2.83% median engagement, square 2.54%, and landscape 2.14% (measured, AuthoredUp Formats, n=368,489 single-image posts, 2026-09). Test portrait layouts for mobile proof; retain wide layouts when cropping would hide material context (directional). A screenshot should show enough surrounding state to substantiate its claim, with private data redacted and comparisons labeled. Use multi-image posts when separate views add evidence, rather than slicing a single graphic mechanically (directional).

Videos of 3+ minutes showed 1.21× reach and 1.17× engagement, compared with 0–30-second clips at 0.96× and 0.91× (measured, AuthoredUp Formats, n=36,946 personal-profile videos, 2026-09). Overall video reach in its update was down 36% year over year (measured, AuthoredUp Formats, n=3M+ personal-profile-post study, 2026-09). Neither observation establishes a universal duration target or a penalty for another platform's provenance.

Use native captioned video when motion or voice helps; the source recommends both (directional; AuthoredUp Formats, 2026-09). Show the operation before any logo introduction. Keep short tasks concise and allow longer demonstrations when necessary; correct captions, make interfaces readable, and supply a transcript or equivalent explanation. Verify current upload limits and caption controls in the publishing account rather than quoting unsupported caps or a compulsory aspect ratio (directional).

### Length

AuthoredUp reports median engagement of 2.10% at 1–400 characters, 2.61% at 1,301–2,000, 2.67% at 2,001–2,500, and 2.62% at 2,501–3,000 (measured, AuthoredUp Length, n=372,126 personal-profile posts, 2026-08). Flypost reports a 13% long-post advantage after matching creator and format, but no measurable text-only effect among 121 paired creators (measured, Flypost, n=83,495 posts from 2,500 B2B creators, 2026-07). Use length as a descriptive test variable. Don't pad a complete idea or claim short posts cannot create conversation (directional).

## Links, accounts, and engagement

Metricool found link-post impressions/interactions at −27%/−20% for profiles and +51%/+41% for Pages (measured, Metricool, n=673,658 posts, 2026-04). These are account-type associations, not randomized root-versus-comment tests. Use a root link when access matters; compare a clearly signposted first-comment alternative only when it still serves the reader (directional). Don't promise that comment placement protects distribution, or that comment links are throttled.

Profiles and Pages had similar impressions per post; profile engagement averaged 2.60%, Page engagement 1.60%, with more profile comments and more Page shares (measured, Metricool, n=673,658 posts from 63,108 accounts, 2026-04). Use the Page for durable product facts and proof that buyers can check. Use people for experience they can personally defend (directional).

Questions correlated with 77.39% more comments; explicit comment CTAs with 80.07% more (measured, Metricool, n=673,658 posts, 2026-04). Don't convert the association into compulsory bait. Ask only when the answer informs the discussion; provide requested resources without forcing public keyword replies (directional).

LinkedIn's March feed update specifically identifies “comment to agree” prompts, repetitive low-substance posts, and video/text mismatches as targets for reduced distribution. This is platform guidance, not an inference from the CTA correlation. Ask for a concrete contribution the author will use; don't manufacture conversations through automation or engagement pods (official, Feed Update, 2026-03).

Buffer's LinkedIn-specific analysis associated author replies with about 30% higher engagement (measured, Buffer Replies, n=72,000 LinkedIn posts from nearly 25,000 profiles, 2025-12). Its later synthesis also reports about 30%, using a broader reply-analysis scope (measured, Buffer State, n=nearly 2M posts from 220,000+ accounts across six platforms, 2026-03). These are different source scopes. Neither proves a reply-count threshold or minute deadline.

Metricool found 50% of impressions arrived within 48 hours (measured, Metricool, n=673,658 posts, 2026-04). Keep answering relevant late comments; don't treat this as a hard lifetime or proof that early responses cannot matter (directional).

Buffer's strongest timing window was 3–8 PM, with Wednesday 4 PM and Friday 3–4 PM highest (measured, Buffer Timing, n=4.8M+ LinkedIn posts, 2026-07). Test in named audience timezones, including alternatives from the account's own history. Rotate global-audience slots and compare comparable formats and goals (directional). Buffer also reports gains beyond a daily post in its LinkedIn frequency analysis (measured, Buffer Timing, n=2M LinkedIn posts, 2026-07); its broader synthesis finds high performers post more frequently, without separating cause from resources or momentum (measured, Buffer State, n=52M+ cross-platform posts, 2026-03). Increase cadence only while substance and response capacity hold (directional).

## Voice, disclosure, and moderation

LinkedIn permits AI help that retains the member's voice and perspective, and reduces wider distribution for apparently AI-generated content without a clear perspective. It acts on scaled automated comments and restatement-only replies (official, LinkedIn Authenticity, 2026-06). Require author review and actual expertise; cosmetic editing alone doesn't solve an empty post (directional).

LinkedIn recommends disclosure of heavy, non-obvious AI reliance; it doesn't require labeling every AI-assisted edit. “Seems like AI slop” feedback may produce a private analytics tip; the tip isn't a policy decision or takedown. LinkedIn urges author review and holds the member responsible for posts (official, LinkedIn AI Help, reviewed 2026-09-05). Review missing substance and facts before cosmetic editing (directional).

For posts exchanged for monetary or other value, LinkedIn requires the Brand Partnership label. The toggle is available only for Public posts and resets Off for each new post; verify the current post rather than relying on an earlier disclosure. Resolve incompatible audience/disclosure choices without hiding the relationship (official, Brand Partnership Help, reviewed 2026-09-05).

Credentialed images and videos can display a Content Credentials icon through the gradual C2PA rollout. LinkedIn cannot identify every AI-generated or modified asset (official, LinkedIn Credentials Help, 2026-09 review, undated). Preserve provenance metadata and inspect what it asserts; a media icon isn't a universal text label. Keep affiliation or sponsorship explicit and use applicable publishing disclosure controls after checking them (directional).

Use these editing lenses without making authorship accusations:

| Lens | Application and evidence boundary |
|---|---|
| Formulaic voice | Remove contrast slogans and repetitive “quietly,” “honestly,” or similar empty emphasis; don't add typos as camouflage (directional; Aborn, 2026-07). |
| Artificial symmetry | Remove filler added to complete a triad; preserve a real three-part list and the author's meaningful punctuation without a quota (directional; Cox, 2026-06). |
| Scripted rhythm | Remove repeated short-line stacks, suspense filler, and obligatory recap endings (directional; Gichigi, 2026-02). |
| Unsupported personal stakes | Ground decisions and costs in interviews or records; never manufacture a failure for relatability (directional; Lees, 2026-08). |
| Neat parables | Preserve confounders and unfinished outcomes. This is a social-writing inference from fiction research that found tidier AI plots (measured, StoryScope preprint, n=61,608 stories, 2026-08); the editing application is directional. |
| Local idiom | Ask a fluent editor to review localized phrasing in context (directional). MIDI found context helped idiom comprehension without removing language disparities (measured, MIDI preprint, n=18 languages, 2026-06); it didn't measure social engagement. |
| Detector scores | Inspect provenance and drafts rather than using a score to certify authorship (directional). Human editing moved detector scores in opposing directions (measured, editing-detector preprint, n=135,389 manuscript pairs across 13 detectors, 2026-08); short social posts weren't studied. |

Assign escalation and out-of-hours coverage according to audience activity; remove fraud links and spam while preserving substantive criticism (directional). Respondology found comments often arrived outside office hours and moderation correlated with less negative feedback (measured, Respondology, n=168.8M comments, multi-platform brand sample, 2026-04). That study isn't a LinkedIn-specific response-time test.

Make the subject understandable quickly without stripping necessary conditions (directional). Reported average daily Android app use was lower for LinkedIn than for the entertainment platforms compared (measured, The Star/DataReportal, n=Android users, sample count unspecified, observation period 2026-02, published 2026-08-19). This measures total daily use, not session duration, attention per post, or an optimal post length. Use external destinations when useful, but don't infer a LinkedIn publishing sequence from global audience totals (directional; DataReportal, 2026-04).
