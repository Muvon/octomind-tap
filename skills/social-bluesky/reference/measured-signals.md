## How to use measured signals

Use these results to choose tests, not to predict an individual post. Keep the sample definition attached to each result. Survey preferences, observed interactions, and conversion outcomes answer different questions.

Bluesky's transparency report records registered-user growth and post volume (official, Transparency report, 2026-01). Registration growth doesn't establish an active audience for the product. Run a community-specific pilot and evaluate actual replies and destination actions before increasing publishing effort (directional).

### Format and response

| Evidence | Bounded interpretation | Writing decision (directional) |
|---|---|---|
| Median interactions: video 5, images 4, links and text 3 each (measured, Buffer engagement, n=Buffer-published Bluesky subset, 2026-03) | Historical observations from Buffer users, primarily the previous calendar year; not a controlled format experiment | Test a real demonstration or useful image when the idea warrants it. |
| Author replies associated with 5% higher Bluesky engagement (measured, Buffer engagement, n=nearly two million cross-platform posts from over 220,000 accounts, 2026-03) | Within-account comparison; replies may follow engagement rather than cause it | Protect time for relevant answers; don't claim Discover rewards questions. |
| Median Bluesky interactions fell to about 4 from about 5 while sampled post volume nearly quadrupled (measured, Buffer engagement, n=Buffer-published Bluesky subset, 2026-03) | Sample conditions changed; this isn't a universal account baseline | Compare with the author's recent, similarly aged posts. |
| Weekly brand engagement reported by 65%; customizable feeds cited by 51%; text-based brand posts preferred for engagement by 45% (measured, Sprout platform survey, n=surveyed Bluesky subset, 2026-03) | Self-reported preferences; no experimental evidence for a specific feed-targeting tactic | Permit straightforward brand text; don't describe the whole platform as hostile to business. |
| Product education ranked highest among desired brand contributions (measured, Sprout statistics, n=surveyed Bluesky subset, 2026-02) | Preference ranking, not a tested launch anatomy | Try a product explanation before a generic announcement. |
| Human-generated content ranked as consumers' top priority (measured, Sprout content strategy, n=consumer survey scope, 2026-09 review; publication undated) | Cross-platform survey, not a Bluesky policy or an AI detector | Preserve the author's judgment; ask for source material instead of inventing personal stakes. |

The Sprout subset sizes aren't supplied in the reviewed claims. Retain that limitation; don't invent respondent counts or assign these percentages to the current active-user population.

### Timing

| Saturday 6 p.m.; Sunday 9 a.m. | Next slots, 6.81% and 6.77% respectively (measured, Buffer timing, n=over three million posts, 2026-08) |
| Weekend daily averages | Saturday 6.17%; Sunday 6.05% (measured, Buffer timing, n=over three million posts, 2026-08) |
| Weekday comparison | Monday 4.32%; Thursday 3.44%, the weekly low (measured, Buffer timing, n=over three million posts, 2026-08) |
| Weekday pattern | Stronger around 6–9 p.m.; quietest around 10 a.m.–1 p.m. (measured, Buffer timing, n=over three million posts, 2026-08) |

Test weekend slots or audience-local weekday evenings; use Monday as a weekday candidate rather than assuming Thursday is best (directional). Record the timezone and staff availability. Don't delay important news just to fit a benchmark (directional).

Buffer describes Following as reverse chronological (directional, Buffer timing, 2026-08). This supports testing audience presence, not a universal post-expiry interval. Don't impose a daily thread ceiling without account evidence.

### Community distribution

Starter-pack inclusion was associated with a substantial short-term repost increase in a study of more than 50,000 packs (measured, Starter-pack study abstract, n=over 50,000 packs, 2026-08). The abstract discusses pre-existing communities; it doesn't establish relationship timing, a matched-control multiplier, or the current pack-size limits. Consider relevant packs without treating them as guaranteed acquisition (directional).

In a monitored panel, 602,186 of 6,661,658 eligible test posts, or 9.04%, appeared in a monitored feed's Top 50 within 24 hours (measured, Custom-feed study abstract, n=5,000 monitored feeds, 2026-08). This is the panel's defined visibility outcome; it doesn't estimate the chance of reaching any Bluesky feed. Record actual inclusion and criteria separately (directional).

Don't equate the same post appearing on multiple surfaces with a proven diffusion path. A repost observed before a count increase doesn't establish how much traffic it caused. Preserve post URLs, collection times, counts, and the baseline comparison (directional).

### Proof from public examples

Sprout describes Barnes & Noble answering an upcoming-book comment with availability information and Yorkshire Tea sharing a customer's tea-travel photo with brief brand commentary (directional, Sprout strategy cases, 2026-07). These are anecdotes, not conversion experiments; the source doesn't establish permission for the photo.

Use those cases as bounded examples of answering an expressed need or adding context to customer content. For your own publishing package, obtain appropriate permission before republishing customer assets, and distinguish a fan's appreciation from evidence of product performance (directional).

### Editorial research boundaries

The current practitioner guidance flags repeated contrast frames, padded lists, uniform staccato rhythm, empty dramatic pivots, excessive em dashes, and recap endings (directional, Aborn, Cox, Gichigi). Use it to edit for the author's voice; none of it establishes a Bluesky ranking mechanism.

Avoid miracle-fix parables as an editorial judgment. StoryScope's findings about explicit themes and tidy plots concern fiction, not social-post performance (directional inference, StoryScope). Preserve unresolved costs and limitations because they belong to the evidence, not because they defeat a detector.

Use authentic source records for specific decisions and first-person stakes (directional, Lees). For localized conversation, have a fluent reviewer check idiom in context (directional inference, MIDI). MIDI studies language comprehension, not Bluesky engagement. Likewise, the editing/detector study concerns academic manuscripts and found conflicting detector responses; don't certify short-post authorship from a detector score (directional inference, Editing study).
